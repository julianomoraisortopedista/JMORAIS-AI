## Scientific database
Build JMORAIS EVIDENCE DATABASE with structured metadata for articles, guidelines, study design, PICO/PICOS, outcomes, limitations, conflicts, funding, evidence level, provenance, PMID/PMCID/DOI, source database, and Vancouver citation.

## Search/RAG
Use hybrid retrieval:
keyword/BM25 + PostgreSQL full-text + metadata filters + vector search + semantic reranking.

## Audit intelligence
Ingest audit documents, extract atomic claims, validate cited sources, determine what the cited source actually supports, retrieve evidence for and against each claim, assess patient applicability, and generate a Claim Evidence Matrix plus an Evidence Pack.

## Evidence integrity
Never cherry-pick. Never fabricate. Clearly label insufficient, conflicting, or unverified evidence.

## Evidence Ledger
Bind every important claim to source, passage/location, identifier, verification status, confidence, and limitations.

## Engineering
Build in phases. Architecture before implementation. Small, tested increments. Do not overengineer.
