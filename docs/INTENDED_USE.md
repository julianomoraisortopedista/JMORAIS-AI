# Intended Use

JMORAIS-AI is evidence governance and clinical decision support for qualified professionals. It is not autonomous diagnosis or treatment and does not replace professional judgment. Only integrity-valid EvidencePackages may enter governed appraisal, and recommendations require authorized human review. Failures of provenance, integrity, authorization, currency or replay fail closed.

This release is pre-production. Patient Context, production API/authentication, RAG, LLM and frontend remain outside the validated boundary.
